# The 90-Minute Focus Matrix — AI Edition
### Coffee Shop Office

Two formats are included:

| File | What it is | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| `prompt.md` | Copy-paste master prompt | Everyone — works in **free** ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini |
| `skill/SKILL.md` | Claude Skill file | Claude paid users & Claude Code users who want it always available |

## Using the prompt (any AI, free plan, no setup)

1. Open `prompt.md`. Select everything **below the horizontal line** and copy it.
2. Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com), [claude.ai](https://claude.ai), or [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) — free accounts work.
3. Start a new chat, paste, and hit send.
4. Answer the six questions. You'll get your Matrix and session plan.
5. After your session, come back to the same chat and say how it went — it will tune your next session.

Tip: bookmark the chat (or in ChatGPT/Gemini, pin it) so your check-ins stay in one thread.

## Installing as a Claude Skill (optional, paid Claude plans)

1. In Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities → Skills** and upload the `skill` folder (or in Claude Code, copy `skill/` to `~/.claude/skills/90-minute-focus-matrix/`).
2. Start any chat and say "run my focus matrix." Claude will find the skill on its own.

## What the system does

1. **Brain dump** — get everything out of your head.
2. **The Matrix** — every task scored on Impact, Urgency, and fit with your *current* energy.
3. **The One** — a single winning task. Multitasking is how 90 minutes disappears.
4. **Sprints** — 3 timed sprints with movement breaks, scaled to the time you really have.
5. **Derailment Defense** — if–then plans for *your* named distractions, plus the Parking Lot.
6. **Shutdown** — wins logged, loose ends parked, next session pre-loaded, reward taken.

Pairs with the printable 90-Minute Focus Matrix planner — use the AI to decide and plan, the paper to run the session with your phone in the other room.
